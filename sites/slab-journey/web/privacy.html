Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Slab Journey (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal data when you use www.slabjourney.com, our native apps (Android, Windows, and iOS when published), and related services. It should be read with our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

Slab Journey provides market price intelligence, portfolio and collection tracking, and collector/shop trading tools. For privacy questions, account deletion, or GDPR requests, email contact@slabjourney.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect the following categories, depending on how you use the service:

  • Account. Email address and authentication data handled by Supabase Auth (we do not store your password in plaintext).
  • Profile. Display name, avatar, and any public profile fields you choose to set.
  • Portfolio, collection, shop, and trades. Cards, grades, quantities, cost basis, notes, listings, shop settings, and request or transaction metadata you create.
  • Waitlist. Email address if you join the launch waitlist (stored with our email provider so we can notify you).
  • Payments (when billing is live). Subscription and entitlement state and payment references. Card numbers are processed by Stripe Checkout on this website — we do not store full card numbers in the apps or our database.
  • Support. Messages, bug reports, and diagnostics you send us.
  • Technical. App or site version, OS, and security logs needed to operate the service. Native apps do not ship product analytics in v1 unless we enable them and update this policy.
  • Website analytics (optional). After you consent, PostHog and Google Analytics may collect usage data such as pages viewed and approximate device/browser information.
  • Preferences. Language, theme, and display currency.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. How we use your data

  • Provide the features you use (account, portfolio, market data, shop, trades, downloads).
  • Authenticate you, keep sessions secure, and prevent abuse.
  • Send transactional email (account confirmation, password reset, waitlist confirmation, welcome).
  • Process Premium subscriptions and service fees on this website when Stripe checkout is enabled.
  • Respond to support and deletion requests, and meet legal obligations.
  • Understand website usage, only if you allow analytics cookies.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on these bases:

  • Contract. To create your account and provide the service you requested.
  • Legitimate interests. Security, fraud prevention, service reliability, and improving the product without overriding your rights.
  • Consent. Optional website analytics (PostHog and Google Analytics) and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time via Cookie Settings in the footer.
  • Legal obligation. Where we must retain or disclose data (for example tax or accounting records once payments are live).

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses a consent banner. You can change your choice later from Cookie Settings in the footer.

  • Necessary. Authentication and session cookies (Supabase) so you can sign in and stay signed in. These are required for the site to work and are always on.
  • Analytics. PostHog and Google Analytics, only after you accept analytics. They help us see how the site is used. Rejecting them does not block core features.

In production the website may also load Vercel Speed Insights to measure performance. That tooling is used to keep the site fast; it is not used to sell advertising profiles.

6. Processors

We use service providers to operate Slab Journey. They process data only as needed to provide their service to us:

ProcessorRole
SupabaseAuthentication, database, and file storage (EU / Frankfurt for this project)
VercelWebsite hosting and the public API
ResendTransactional and waitlist email
PostHogWebsite product analytics, only with cookie consent
Google AnalyticsWebsite analytics, only with cookie consent
StripeCheckout and billing when payments are enabled on the website
PriceChartingMarket price feed — not your personal account data
FrankfurterPublic ECB-based FX rates for display currency conversion

We do not currently use Sentry or similar error-tracking on the website. If that changes, we will update this list.

7. When we share data

We share personal data with the processors above, and if required by law or to protect the service (for example to investigate abuse). We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own marketing. Public profile fields you choose to publish can be seen by other users when those features are enabled.

8. International transfers

Application data for this project is stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Some processors (for example Vercel, analytics, or Stripe when live) may process data in other regions. Where a transfer outside the EEA/UK is needed, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the processor’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

9. Retention

We keep account and product data while your account is active. Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or we close the waitlist. After a verified deletion request we delete or anonymize personal data within 30 days, except where law requires longer retention (for example payment records once billing is live). Backups may lag for a short period before they expire.

10. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another region with similar laws, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and you may withdraw consent for analytics cookies. Email contact@slabjourney.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

11. Account deletion

To delete your account and associated personal data, email contact@slabjourney.com from the same address as the account (or otherwise prove ownership) with the subject line Account deletion request. After we verify the request we delete or anonymize auth, profile, portfolio, collection, shop, trade, notification, and entitlement data we hold, plus storage objects you uploaded (such as avatars). The native apps also offer Account → Legal → request deletion, which opens the same email path.

12. Children

You must be at least 13 years old, or older where required by local law. Slab Journey is not directed at children. If we learn we have collected data from a child below that age, we will delete it.

13. Native apps

This policy covers the Android, Windows, and iOS clients as well as the website. Store listings and the in-app Legal section link here. Native v1 does not include product analytics unless we enable them and update this policy the same day.

14. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS in transit, hashed credentials via our auth provider, and access controls (including row-level security on application data). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong unique password.

15. Changes

We will post material updates on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Where appropriate we will also email the address on your account. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy where the law allows.

16. Contact

Privacy and data-protection requests: contact@slabjourney.com.
