Our story

Built by collectors,
for collectors.

Slab Journey started with a frustration every serious Pokémon collector knows — you open five tabs to guess what a card is worth, then still miss the grade that actually moves the price. There had to be a better way.

The problem

Grade is the number. Most tools hide it.

A 1st Edition Charizard in PSA 10 is not the same asset as a PSA 7. Raw listings, sold comps, and “market price” blur that gap — and collectors undersell or overpay because of it.

Shops have the opposite problem: inventory, buy requests, and safekeeping live in spreadsheets. We wanted one place for the book and the counter.

The product

Live prices. A real portfolio. Shops that pay their way.

We track 20,000+ English Pokémon cards across 170+ sets with live PriceCharting prices. Free accounts keep a book of 50 cards. Plus and Slab'Em All raise how many portfolios you can keep live at once, how much daily history you see, and — on Slab'Em All — drop the shop trade fee to 0%. Shops start on Starter or Pro. There is no free shop plan.

What we stand for

Grades are the market

The same card can be worth 200× more in PSA 10 than in PSA 7. We show Ungraded through PSA 10 so you price the slab you actually hold.

One price source, named

Every figure comes from PriceCharting — not a silent blend of five shops. You always know what you are looking at.

Built by collectors
