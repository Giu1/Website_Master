The app

Tap through it

The phone on this page is the Android app, rebuilt in HTML from its Compose code: same colours, layout and wording. Tap a card, switch grades, add it to your portfolio, and use the bar at the bottom like you would on the phone.

Card prices come from the app's own screenshots. Collection, portfolio and community numbers are demo data, because the real ones lived behind sign-in.

One codebase for the app, one for the web

App · Android & Windows

  • Kotlin Multiplatform with Compose Multiplatform, so one UI ran on Android, Windows and iOS
  • Supabase (Postgres and Auth) over Ktor, with Koin for wiring
  • Daily PriceCharting and Cardmarket prices, converted to euros on the device
  • Portfolio with profit and loss, set tracker, trades with shop safekeeping, card scan
  • English, Portuguese and Spanish

Website

  • Next.js 14 and Tailwind, light and dark themes
  • Live grade price table, plans and a waitlist for paid tiers
  • Accounts on the same Supabase project as the app
  • Signed Windows installer downloads behind an allowlist
  • Cookie consent with analytics only after opt-in
