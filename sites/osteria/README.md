# Osteria Corvo

Fictional restaurant in Príncipe Real, Lisboa. Paper, ink and wine red; Fraunces + Libre Franklin. EN + PT-PT. One page.

- **Hero** — a live line with tonight's seats left, or the next free table (click it to preselect that night and sitting).
- **Tonight, in five chapters** — the week's set menu, one course per chapter with allergen tags. The **wine pairing** switch reveals a glass under each course and updates the price.
- **Kitchen** and **cellar** — wines from fictional producers, filterable by red, white, orange and sparkling, by the glass and the bottle.
- **Reservations** — guests (1–8), a night (closed Sunday and Monday), a sitting (19:00 or 21:30) with live seats left, name, phone and dietary chips. Seats come from a stable demo calendar minus reservations made in this browser (`localStorage["corvo-reservations"]`). "Your reservations" lists them with cancel, which frees the seats again.

Courses (`COURSES`) and wines (`WINES`) live in `js/main.js`. Photographs from Unsplash.
