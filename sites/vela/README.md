# Vela

Fictional perfume house with one fragrance, N°07 Night Bloom. Night purple and gold; Bodoni Moda + Jost. EN + PT-PT. One page.

- **Hero** — an original SVG bottle (no photographed or branded bottles). Its liquid sways continuously, tilts with the pointer, and takes the colour of the selected note layer.
- **Notes** — top, heart and base as tabs (arrow keys work); each shows its notes with intensity bars.
- **Wear clock** — a slider from "just sprayed" to twelve hours; the notes still on skin at that hour light up, and the bottle follows the dominant layer.
- **Making** — three unbranded photographs.
- **Formats** — sample, travel and bottle as radio cards with a drawn vial each; the request form stays on the page.

Note layers (`LAYERS`), their timing (`LIFE`) and formats (`FORMATS`) live in `js/main.js`.
