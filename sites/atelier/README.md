# Atelier Moura

Fictional bespoke tailor on Rua dos Fanqueiros, Lisboa. Chalk paper, black thread and a yellow tape measure; Instrument Serif + Hanken Grotesk. EN + PT-PT. One page (it used to be the sales hub; the work, offer and contact pages are gone).

- **Tape measure** across the top reads 0–150 cm as you scroll down the page.
- **Designer** — an SVG jacket redrawn from your choices: 8 cloths (each weave is an SVG pattern: twill, chalk stripe, herringbone, Prince of Wales check, linen, flannel, tweed), notch / peak / shawl lapel, one or two buttons or double-breasted, flap / jetted / patch pockets, four linings and a monogram on the inside label. Live price, weeks, fittings and hours of hand work. "Keep this design" saves it (`localStorage["moura-design"]`; the working draft is `moura-design-draft`).
- **Process** — five steps from measuring to collection, each with a photo.
- **Bunch book** — the same cloth list, filtered by season; "Use this cloth" sends it to the designer.
- **Fittings** — first visit (60 min), fitting (45) or collection (20); Tuesday to Saturday, lunch kept free, a stable made-up diary and your own visits taken out. Your kept design comes along. Visits get an `AM-XXXX` code and can be cancelled (`localStorage["moura-visits"]`).

Cloths, options, prices and steps live at the top of `js/main.js`.
